Good to know

Everything is processed in your browser. No text is uploaded to our server. EPUB and PDF imports require internet the first time to load their open-source parsers; pasted text and TXT work without them. Text-based PDFs work best; scanned/image-only PDFs require OCR, which this version does not include. DRM-locked books cannot be opened. On iPhone/iPad, Apple's browser speech system may pause or stop when the screen locks or the app goes into the background. Keep the app open for reliable listening.
